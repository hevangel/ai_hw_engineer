The change implements both variants in one `intel_8253_8254` module selected by `IS_8254`, and resolves all five findings from the prior review. Packed-BCD Mode 3, low-GATE Mode 4 recovery, pre-arm Mode 1/5 ordering, and the missing behavioral oracles now have aligned RTL and directed checks; formal and reports now limit proof claims to the implemented obligations.

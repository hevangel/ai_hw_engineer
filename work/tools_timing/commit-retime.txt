system/apple_ii: sample socket reads at the phi2 falling edge

The directed socket test sampled read data 470 ns after the phi2 rising
edge, 18 ns before the phase ends. With per-part datasheet delays the DRAM
drives the bus at +469 ns (typical) and +472 ns (maximum), so that fixed
sample landed 1-3 ns inside the valid window: the maximum-delay run failed
on a timing coincidence in the harness, not on a board fault.

Sample at the phi2 falling edge instead, where a 6502 latches read data,
and print how long each DRAM bank took to drive the bus. Phi2 high lasts
488.9 ns, so the measured margin is 60 ns at legacy delays, 20 ns at
typical and 17 ns at maximum.

With the socket result now meaningful at maximum delays, run_timing_sim.sh
covers that test at both typical and maximum instead of typical only.

The pixel-exact frame comparison stays on the legacy board, and the reason
is measured rather than assumed: at datasheet delays composite_sync is
produced after a deeper gate chain than the counter outputs the oracle
derives it from, so a same-instant comparison fails, and no constant dot
shift reconciles them (best agreement is 70% of the frame across shifts of
-4..+4 samples). A delay-faithful video check needs an edge-time comparison
rather than a per-dot sample.

Verification (Icarus 12.0-3, apple-ii image), same authored CPU and
unmodified Monitor ROM throughout:
- run_timing_sim.sh passes: socket wiring and Monitor boot at typical and
  maximum delays, measured arrivals +469 ns and +472 ns.
- run_board_sim.sh with SIMULATOR=iverilog passes on the legacy board,
  arrivals +429 ns.
- tb_rev0_boot.sv is unchanged, so the legacy frame gate and the nine
  video diagnostics are unaffected.

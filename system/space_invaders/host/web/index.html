Taito 1978, licensed by Midway — functional reconstruction on the repository's Intel 8080 RTL. Original ROM, fetched and hash-checked at run time; two 60 Hz RST interrupts, hardware shift register, watchdog. Cycle-accurate pin timing, discrete analog sound and the cocktail flip are outside the model.

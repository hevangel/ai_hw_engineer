Nintendo 1983 (Famicom) / 1985 (NES) — dot-level reconstruction on the
repository's Ricoh 2A03 CPU/APU and 2C02 PPU RTL with NROM, MMC1, UxROM and
CNROM cartridge boards. Software comes from the hash-pinned public test-ROM
archive, or any mapper 0–3 iNES file placed in work/roms/.
Composite video encoding and the analog audio filters are approximated in
the browser.

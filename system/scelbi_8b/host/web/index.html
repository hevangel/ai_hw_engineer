The 8008 talks to the world through a bit-banger serial card: nine INPs per received character, ten OUTs per transmitted one. Type and the machine hears you at 110-baud rhythm.

About this machine

The SCELBI 8B (Scelbi Computer Consulting, 1975) put an Intel 8008 and up to 16 KiB of memory behind a serial console. This reconstruction boots the authentic SCELBAL interpreter binary (SCELBI's SCientific ELementary BAsic, floating point, 6 digits) from the Scelbi archive, entered through the very bit-banger protocol SIMH models.

Press LOAD *STARS* for the 1974 People's Computer Company guessing game: guess the number from 1 to 100, and the stars tell you how close you are. Seven stars — you are burning.

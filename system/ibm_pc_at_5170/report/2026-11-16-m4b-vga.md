# M4b part 1: VGA card on the AT — 2026-11-16

## Result

With an I/O channel and the IBM PS/2 Display Adapter (VGA) in a slot, the
unchanged IBM AT REV1 BIOS (01/10/84) and the card's own BIOS run
16,294,929 instructions of power-on self-test and stop at `F000:1454`,
after the BIOS's first read of port 1F7h (fixed-disk status) at POST
checkpoint 3Ch. That is the same instruction at which the board without a
card and the EGA machine stop, so the boundary has not moved. The machine's
display is now VGA: the last video frame is 720 x 400 and reads
`00640 KB OK`, ` 301-Keyboard Error`, ` 601-Diskette Error`, the same text
and the same reasons as on the EGA machine (no keyboard is plugged in and
no diskette adapter is modeled). Nothing boots.

The VGA BIOS's power-on self-test completes on the machine and sets its
80-column 720 x 400 text mode; this is inferred from the screen. This
report makes no further claim about what the self-test checked; the card's
own report,
[design/ibm_vga](../../../design/ibm_vga/report/final_report.md), describes
the self-test.

## What was added

- **`src/ibm_pc_at_5170_vga.sv`.** The machine top: the unchanged board,
  the channel (`at_io_channel`) and the IBM VGA card from
  [`design/ibm_vga`](../../../design/ibm_vga/) (IBM PS/2 Display Adapter,
  256 KB, DAC, adapter BIOS ROM in the 32 KB window C0000h-C7FFFh of which
  24 KB is ROM). The card's IRQ2 goes to the board's IRQ 9. A colour analog
  monitor is attached (`load_i` = 111). The card is enabled by the ROM
  through ports 46E8h and 102h (see the card's specification).
- **Clocks.** The card's clock is twice the processor clock and stands for
  48 MHz on the AT19 time base, because the card needs a clock at least as
  fast as its 28.322 MHz oscillator. The 25.175 MHz and 28.322 MHz
  oscillators are clock enables from phase accumulators. This extends
  ASSUMPTION AT19 (the EGA machine's card clock stands for 24 MHz); see
  [the specification](../spec/spec.md).
- **Bench** (`tb/ibm_pc_at_5170_vga_tb.sv`) and CMOS image. The same image
  `tb/cmos_ega.hex` is used (equipment byte 01h: display with its own
  BIOS).
- **Runner** (`tb/post_runner.cpp`). Built with `-DAT_VGA` it drives the VGA
  machine. The frame holds the DAC's 6-bit codes scaled to 8 bits per gun.
- **Scripts.** `scripts/run_post_card.sh ega|vga /path/to/atbios.bin` is now
  the one regression for either card; `scripts/run_post_ega.sh` and
  `scripts/run_post_vga.sh` are wrappers. New: `scripts/expected_stop_vga.txt`,
  `tb/expected_screen_vga.txt` and the VGA machine's synthesis in
  `scripts/run_synth.sh`.
- **`scripts/check_frame.py`** now also reads 720 x 400 VGA text: 9 x 16
  cells from the ROM's 8 x 16 font, the ROM's table of replacement glyphs
  for 9-dot modes (19 characters) and the ninth-column rule.

## How the new parts are checked

- **Screen.** Every 9 x 16 cell of the last frame must be one glyph of the
  font in the VGA ROM (with the ROM's replacement glyphs and the
  ninth-column rule), and the decoded rows must equal
  `tb/expected_screen_vga.txt`. This checks the display path (BIOS character
  writes, video memory, font, attribute logic, DAC and timing) against the
  ROM's own font, not against the card model. It is a check of one frame.
- **80286.** The Unicorn replay covers both BIOSes and needed no change for
  the VGA ROM. No event counters were collected for this run, so none are
  stated here.
- **8042 and MC146818.** Replayed into the UPI-42 model and the MC146818
  model as in M3 and M4a; see the table.
- **8259s.** Still checked only by the BIOS's own tests and by the vectors
  and in-service values seen.

## Limits

Every limit of the [M4a report](2026-11-16-m4a-io-channel-and-ega.md) still
applies:

- Channel timing is not the real board's (AT18); no card stretches a cycle
  and I/O CH RDY is not used.
- The time base is virtual (AT19). The 8042 and the MC146818 run four times
  fast relative to video.
- One scenario for the VGA machine: no added wait states.
- No keyboard device, no diskette adapter and no fixed-disk adapter, so POST
  ends in the two errors and the run stops at the fixed-disk probe. Nothing
  boots.
- The 8259s have no independent model.
- Unicorn does not check segment limits or rights on accesses that do not
  fault.
- The display is checked as the text of one frame, not continuously.
- No whole-machine comparison with an independent emulator.
- The 80287 is not fitted, NMI is not connected, and the other M3 limits
  stand.

VGA-specific:

- The VGA ROM image could not be cross-checked against an IBM listing (see
  the [card's README](../../../design/ibm_vga/README.md) and
  [specification](../../../design/ibm_vga/spec/spec.md)).
- Graphics modes and the DAC are not exercised by this POST run; only the
  card's own suite covers them.

## Reproduce

```sh
ROM=$(sh system/ibm_pc_at_5170/scripts/get_bios.sh)
sh system/ibm_pc_at_5170/scripts/run_post_vga.sh "$ROM"
sh system/ibm_pc_at_5170/scripts/run_synth.sh
```

`run_post_vga.sh` calls `run_post_card.sh vga`. It fetches and hash-checks
the VGA ROM with the card design's `scripts/get_rom.py` into the ignored
`work/` folder and writes the frame to `work/post_vga/frame.ppm` and
`frame.png`. `scripts/run_all.sh` does not run it.

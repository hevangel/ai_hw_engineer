# Formal plan

Initial reset assumption, arbitrary legal-width host/media inputs and strobes. BMC and unbounded PDR prove command/result index bounds, stable result index when no result read is accepted, DRQ gating, no pending data in result, reserved status bits, bounded sector/byte counters and recalibration counter bounds. The two stronger invariants `byte_count <= sector_size` and `sector_size <= 8192` imply the byte-count bound; a redundant separate assertion is unnecessary. Formal does not prove complete command semantics, byte contents, IRQ acknowledgement priority or electrical timing.

Four cover tasks supply separate legal prefixes for DMA read, DMA write, invalid command/result, seek step/SIS and Specify plus non-DMA service. Safety tasks do not inherit this cover stimulus. Eight goals must be reached within 80 cycles. `MS_US=1` and `RQM_DELAY_US=1` accelerate timers in the harness. Simulation checks pulse counts, direction, timing, held strobes and full result drain. Codec combinational correctness is exhaustively tested against external vectors and CRC known answers. Proof claims are limited to listed invariants and their accelerated parameters, not the physical floppy channel.

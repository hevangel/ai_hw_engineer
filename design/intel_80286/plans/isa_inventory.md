For memory ESC, next extend the bus owner with an independently tested
processor-extension channel and connect PEREQ/PEACK# through the native wrapper.
The channel needs source-backed transfer direction and bounds, even/odd word
sequencing, indivisible ownership, LOCK/HOLD priority and fault ordering before
memory commands can safely retire. Use the D8 physical captures for command and
pointer order, but do not treat them as evidence of an attached 80287's PEREQ
behavior or extrapolate their command encoding to other opcodes without further
evidence. Exercise real extension software before signing off the channel.

Undocumented instructions and aliases are not implicitly inferred from modern
x86 emulators. Any decision about them requires an explicit source and scope
entry; A15 is based on pinned physical Harris captures and remains stepping-
bounded. Current-source physical replay, the 36-task formal audit, 99,650
independent vectors and historical software replay pass. The selected physical
adapter covers 68,510 records, including the F6/F7 register subset; its 7,490
memory records remain outside RTL replay. These results do not close the
remaining ISA, protected-mode or native-interface rows.
The [full completion ledger](completion_requirements.md) additionally tracks
native pins, platform/software, verification environment and review gates.

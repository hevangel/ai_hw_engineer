# Sony S-DSP

The S-DSP is the sound generator of the Super Famicom / SNES. It plays eight
voices of BRR-compressed samples from the 64 KB audio RAM, each with pitch,
4-point gaussian interpolation, ADSR or gain envelopes, pitch modulation and
noise, mixes them in stereo, and adds an 8-tap FIR echo whose buffer also
lives in audio RAM. It outputs one 16-bit stereo sample every 32 µs
(32 kHz).

**First introduced: 1990**, in the Super Famicom released in Japan on
21 November 1990 ([Super Famicom history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Nintendo_Entertainment_System#History)).
Behaviour follows [fullsnes](https://problemkaputt.de/fullsnes.htm) and
blargg's cycle-accurate description of the 32-step per-sample schedule.

This original model follows that fixed 32-phase schedule: each phase is a
short list of micro-operations generated by `scripts/gen_tables.py`, and the
gaussian table is generated from its published formula and cross-checked
against the 512-entry hardware dump.

Read the [specification and assumptions](spec/spec.md),
[implementation plan](plans/implementation_plan.md) and
[verification report](report/final_report.md). `scripts/run_all.sh` runs
lint, the table check and synthesis.

## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x92 — Divide-by-12 counter (÷2 and ÷6)

Two independent sections: a ÷2 toggle (`q_a`) and a 3-bit mod-6 counter
(`q_b`, `q_c`, `q_d`). For full ÷12, cascade `q_a` into `clk_b`.

Reset is asynchronous when `r0_1 & r0_2 = 1`.


## Reviewed digital contract

Clock A and clock B advance their independent sections on falling edges, matching the TI 7490/92/93 counter tables. No propagation delays are inserted; cascade QA into clock B externally.

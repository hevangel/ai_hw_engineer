## Reference interface notes (MIT baseline)

# ttl_74x908 — Dual 2-input NAND relay driver

Two NAND gates per package, intended to drive small relays at high
voltage and current. Only the logical function (NAND) is modeled; the
30 V / 250 mA drive characteristics are physical-only.

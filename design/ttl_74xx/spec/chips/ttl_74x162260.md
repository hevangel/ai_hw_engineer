# 74x162260: source-backed wide registered transceiver/latch

**First introduced:** Unknown; exact launch year has not been established.
**Manufacturer reference:** [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74alvch162260.pdf).
**Status:** implemented.

The model covers the digital function table for the TI device family selected by the reference. This function provides a wide, independently controlled bus storage/transfer building block for board-level 74-series logic. See the [library contract](../spec.md), [extension plan](../../plans/extended_plan.md), and [provenance](../../references/README.md).

## Source-backed behavior

A is 12 bits; B1 and B2 are separate 12-bit ports. `sel` chooses 1B (1) or 2B (0) for B-to-A. The four active-high latch enables independently control A-to-1B, A-to-2B, 1B-to-A, and 2B-to-A storage. OEA, OE1B, OE2B are active-low and independently gate their physical ports.

Tri-state outputs are represented by explicit drive flags. A data output is forced to zero while its drive flag is low; zero in that condition does not mean that the physical bus is driven low. Output data is otherwise the selected real-time or stored logic value described by the manufacturer table.

## Modeling assumptions and limits

- `ASSUMPTION:` Power-up register values are unspecified by the cited source; the RTL does not initialize registers.
- `ASSUMPTION:` Analog bus voltage, output resistance, propagation/setup/hold timing, bus contention, and the 162260 input bus-hold cells are outside this pure-digital model.
- Native output-enable, direction, latch, select, and per-section clock controls are retained; each 8-bit section is separately modeled where the datasheet shows separate section controls.

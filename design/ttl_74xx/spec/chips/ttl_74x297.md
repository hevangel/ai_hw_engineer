## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x297 — Digital PLL loop filter

Up/down counter that approximates an analog loop filter for digital PLL
applications. `inc` increases the count (toward a higher VCO frequency);
`dec` decreases it. The 8-bit `filter_out` would drive the VCO control
input.


## Reviewed digital contract

The original generic eight-bit filter is replaced. The model contains the programmable K counter (code 0 inhibits; code 1..15 gives 2**3..2**17), increment/decrement transition insertion/deletion and both phase detectors. The ECPD table is read from the scan: falling B sets, falling A2 resets, rising inputs hold. **ASSUMPTION:** `clk` resolves all native clock/phase edges; at most one same-direction K overflow may await service at the I/D circuit. Simultaneous opposite phase-detector falls are outside the contract. `rst_n` explicitly represents power-up reset. This normalized interface preserves the functional event/rate relationship; the internal TI pipeline latency and native I/D waveform placement are not claimed. Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/sn74ls297, SDLS155 pages 2–4.

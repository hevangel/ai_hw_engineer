# 74x4060: 14-stage binary counter with oscillator interface

First introduced: Unknown. [TI SN74HC4060 SCLS161D](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc4060.pdf), December 1982,
revised September 2003, PDF pages 1–2. These dates document publication, not
confirmed first shipment. SHA-256: `e0d763603b69eb80b37afcc26f4378c2aa6d9e5b6d27c0a555c2c0397af0823a`.
The part combined clock generation and multiple binary divider taps on a small
board. This model implements its externally clocked digital use.

## Native digital contract and plan

`clki` maps CLKI pin 11 and `clr` active-high CLR pin 12. A falling CLKI edge
increments the 14-stage counter; rising edges hold. CLR asynchronously resets
all stages and dominates clock edges. Unreset initial state is unspecified.
`q[9:0]`, in increasing bit order, maps QD,QE,QF,QG,QH,QI,QJ,QL,QM,QN.
These are counter stages 4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,13,14. Stage 11 has no native output.
The corresponding DIP pins are 7,5,4,6,14,13,15,1,2,3.

From the positive-logic diagram on page 2, pin 9 `clko` is
`NOT clr AND clki`, and pin 10 `clko_n` is `clr OR NOT clki`. Thus CLR high
forces CLKO low and its complementary output high, as the source prose states.
The pin-10 branch leaves the NAND before the inverter; pin 9 follows the inverter.
The model supplies no autonomous oscillator. Drive CLKI with the board's digital
clock. Analog RC/crystal networks and ripple propagation are omitted.

## Native digital contract

The A port has split input and output pins: `ai_in[7:0]` observes A-input pins
AI1..AI8 in bits 0..7, and `ao_out[7:0]` drives A-output pins AO1..AO8 in bits
0..7. `b_in[7:0]` observes resolved BTL pins B1..B8 in bits 0..7 (1 = line
released by every driver, 0 = asserted low by at least one driver).
`ao_drive` is the scalar bank-enable flag for the single OEA-gated A-output
bank. `b_drive[7:0]` carries per-pin open-collector sink flags following the
16246 precedent (flag 1 = pin actively sinking its line low, flag 0 = pin
released); `b_out[7:0]` is always zero and board adapters ignore it.

`oeb` (active high) and `oeb_n` (active low) jointly enable the B-port
drivers; `oea` (active high) enables the AO drivers. This reproduces every row
of the function table:

| oeb | oeb_n | oea | function                      |
|-----|-------|-----|-------------------------------|
| 0   | x     | 0   | isolation                     |
| x   | 1     | 0   | isolation                     |
| 0   | x     | 1   | B-bar data to AO bus          |
| x   | 1     | 1   | B-bar data to AO bus          |
| 1   | 0     | 0   | A-bar data to B bus           |
| 1   | 0     | 1   | A-bar to B bus, B-bar to AO   |

Data is inverted on both paths, per the description: the B port reflects the
inverse of the data at the A-input pins (`b_drive[i] = oeb & ~oeb_n & ai_in[i]`,
so an AI pin high sinks its B line), and the A outputs reflect the inverse of
the data at the B port (`ao_out = ~b_in` while `oea=1`). The two directions
are fully independent and combinational; the device has no storage.

ASSUMPTION: Externally resolved powered logic; a released B line is observed
high only because the board's bias network/pullups resolve it before the pin.
The IEEE 1149.1 pins allocated on the package are no-connects in this
product-preview version (TMS and TCK not connected, TDI shorted to TDO) and
are omitted. BIAS Vcc, BG Vcc, BG GND, the B-port bias network, the 2 ns
minimum edge rate, 100 mA sink current, Vcc < 2.5 V power-off isolation,
thresholds and timing are analog/electrical behavior and are omitted.

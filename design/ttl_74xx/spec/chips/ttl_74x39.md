## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x39 — Quad 2-input NAND, OC 60 mA, alternate pinout

Logic identical to `ttl_74x00`. OC + 60 mA drive not modeled. The 74x39
has a different DIP pinout than 74x38, but with vector ports this matters
only at physical pin assignment time.

# 74x658: octal inverting parity bus transceiver

**First introduced:** Unknown; exact launch year has not been established.
**Manufacturer reference:** [TI High-Speed CMOS Logic Data Book (1984), SN74HC658/659](https://archive.org/details/bitsavers_tidataBookogicDataBook_23574286/page/n395), printed pages 3-350–3-351.
**Status:** implemented; source reviewed.

## Function

The SN74HC658 is an asynchronous octal bidirectional bus transceiver with parity outputs. `GBA_N` is the active-low enable for B-to-A transfer and `GAB` is the active-high enable for A-to-B transfer. Therefore `gba_n=0,gab=0` transfers B to A; `gba_n=1,gab=1` transfers A to B; `gba_n=1,gab=0` isolates both buses; and `gba_n=0,gab=1` enables both directions concurrently. The native table permits both transfer controls to be active at once.

Both data directions invert the transferred byte. `APO` is even parity over A plus `API` and is driven when A-to-B transfer is enabled. `BPO` is even parity over B plus `BPI` and is driven when B-to-A transfer is enabled. An even number of highs produces H; an odd number produces L. Each parity output is high impedance whenever its corresponding direction is disabled.

## Local model contract

The normalized ports preserve the native controls, buses, parity inputs and parity outputs. High impedance is represented by `a_drive`, `b_drive`, `apo_drive`, and `bpo_drive`; disabled data/parity values are zero and must be ignored when the corresponding drive flag is low. The model omits timing, electrical loading, voltage/current details, and the datasheet-described internal active pull-ups/pull-downs and bus-hold behavior on the transceiver inputs. This is a settled ideal-digital model; there is no storage or power-up state.

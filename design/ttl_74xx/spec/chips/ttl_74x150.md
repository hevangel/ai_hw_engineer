## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x150 — 16-to-1 multiplexer with enable, inverting output

Selects one of 16 data inputs. Output is inverted (active-low). When
`g_n=1`, output is forced high (i.e., reset to its "inactive" state).

## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x283 — 4-bit binary full adder (improved pinout)

Logic identical to `ttl_74x83`. The 74x283 has Vcc and GND on the
standard pin-14 / pin-7 corners; the 74x83 does not. Newer designs
prefer the 74x283. The RTL interface is the same.

# 74x16601: 18-bit universal bus transceiver (registered, active high clock)

First introduced: Unknown; document publication is not a confirmed product launch.
Manufacturer source: [SN74ABT16601](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74abt16601.pdf), pages 1–3.
SHA-256: `a0a3c1926b51d4b6b1c13b10ee741dfa4716c0243413b77652b47a5c63fda6ee`.

This board-level bus building block supports backplane, memory and peripheral
interconnects. Its digital storage, transfer and enable functions remain useful
independently of electrical family or packaging.

## Logical interface contract

The two 18-bit transfer directions have independent storage, clocks, latch
enables and output enables. `a_in[17:0]` and `b_in[17:0]` map package A1..A18 and
B1..B18 observations; `b_out` contains A-to-B data and `a_out` contains B-to-A data.
`le_ab=1` and `le_ba=1` make the respective storage transparent at either clock
level. Otherwise the **rising edge** captures
data, and both stable levels/opposite edges hold.

`oe_ab_n=0` enables B outputs. `oe_ba_n=0` enables A outputs.
The two positive drive indications are independent, including when both enables
are active. Independent clken_ab_n/clken_ba_n pins inhibit edge capture when high; transparency remains active regardless of clock-enable.
For qualified LE transitions the clock is high
before LE falls. Tests settle inputs/modes and close transparency at that level.

ASSUMPTION: Data/control setup and hold obey the manufacturer table. Analog
voltage conversion, drive strength, passive damping and package delays are omitted.
Three-state outputs are split into data and positive drive indications; masked
zero data is ignored whenever drive is low. Unreset storage starts unspecified.

## Reviewed digital contract

`drive_en` is the per-bit output-drive mask: 1 drives, 0 releases. The data output keeps its deterministic masked-zero value while disabled. For a resolved external bus use `drive_en[i] ? data[i] : 1'bz`. This avoids confusing a disabled output with a driven zero.

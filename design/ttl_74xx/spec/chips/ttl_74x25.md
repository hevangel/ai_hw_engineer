## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x25 — Dual 4-input NOR with strobe

Digital function identical to `ttl_74x23` without the expander interface.
`y[i] = g[i] & ~(a[i] | b[i] | c[i] | d[i])`.


## Local contract corrections

The strobe follows TI volume 2, page 3-123: G low forces Y high; G high enables the NOR function. The original baseline had the wrong polarity.

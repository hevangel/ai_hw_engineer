## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x165 — 8-bit PISO shift register

`sh_ld_n=0`: parallel load `sr <= p` on posedge.
`sh_ld_n=1`: shift right `sr <= {ser, sr[7:1]}`.

Serial output `q_h` is the LSB of `sr` (the bit shifted out first); `q_h_n`
is its complement.


## Load, shift and clock polarity correction

Parallel bit 7 is the first serial output; serial input enters bit 0. `clk_inh` OR-gates the native clock. Change inhibit while clk is high to avoid generating a clock transition. The 74165 parallel load is asynchronous and remains transparent while sh_ld_n=0, including changes on P without a clock. The older reversed serial ordering and synchronous-only 165 description are superseded.

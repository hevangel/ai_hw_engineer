## Reference interface notes (MIT baseline)

# ttl_74x8154 — Dual 16-bit counters with output registers

Two independent 16-bit up-counters with their own clock, enable, and async
clear, plus an output register per side sampled on `reg_clk` for stable
parallel readout.

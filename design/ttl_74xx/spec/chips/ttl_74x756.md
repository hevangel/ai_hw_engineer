package-pin replica. Electrical families, propagation delays and analog effects

## Local open-collector correction

Disabled output banks release the collectors, represented as logical ones with
a pull-up. Board-level resolution is wired AND. Baseline zero-on-disable behavior
incorrectly sank the disabled bus and is superseded. All 256 data values and all
four bank-enable combinations are tested.

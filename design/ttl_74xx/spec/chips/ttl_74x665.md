# 74x665: octal non-inverting parity bus transceiver

**First introduced:** Unknown; exact launch year has not been established.
**Manufacturer reference:** [TI High-Speed CMOS Logic Data Book (1984), SN74HC664/665](https://archive.org/details/bitsavers_tidataBookogicDataBook_23574286/page/n409), printed pages 3-364–3-365.
**Status:** implemented; source reviewed.

## Function

The SN74HC665 is an asynchronous octal bidirectional bus transceiver with parity outputs. The active-low `G_N` input enables the device. With `g_n=0`, `dir=0` transfers B to A and `dir=1` transfers A to B. With `g_n=1`, both buses and both parity outputs are isolated regardless of `dir`.

The 74HC665 transfers data without inversion in either direction. `APO` is even parity over A plus `API` and is driven in A-to-B mode. `BPO` is even parity over B plus `BPI` and is driven in B-to-A mode. An even number of highs produces H; an odd number produces L. The inactive parity output is high impedance.

## Local model contract

The normalized ports preserve the native enable, direction, bus, and parity controls. High impedance is represented by `a_drive`, `b_drive`, `apo_drive`, and `bpo_drive`; disabled data/parity values are zero and must be ignored when the corresponding drive flag is low. The model omits timing, electrical loading, voltage/current details, and the datasheet-described internal active pull-ups/pull-downs and bus-hold behavior on the transceiver inputs. This is a settled ideal-digital model; there is no storage or power-up state.

# 74x162344: 1-bit-to-4-bit address driver

**First introduced:** Unknown; the exact launch year has not been established.
**Manufacturer reference:** [TI SN74ALVCH162344 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74alvch162344.pdf).
**Status:** implemented.

The SN74ALVCH162344 is a noninverting address driver with eight independent input channels and four buffered output copies per channel. Each active-low output enable controls two adjacent input channels (eight output pins). See the [library contract](../spec.md), [extension plan](../../plans/extended_plan.md), and [provenance](../../references/README.md).

## Source-backed behavior

The function table on PDF page 2 defines each A-to-B path as noninverting: with OE low, the output follows its A input; with OE high, the output is high impedance. The positive-logic diagram on PDF page 3 shows OE1 controlling channels 1 and 2, OE2 channels 3 and 4, OE3 channels 5 and 6, and OE4 channels 7 and 8.

Port vectors are indexed from zero. `a[i]` corresponds to A(i+1), and `b[4*i +: 4]` is the four B outputs for that input. `oe_n[0]` through `oe_n[3]` correspond to OE1 through OE4. `drive[4*i +: 4]` indicates which copies are actively driven. While drive is low, `b` is masked to zero; this is a digital stand-in for Z and does not mean the physical pin is driven low.

## Modeling assumptions and limits

- `ASSUMPTION:` Power-up behavior and any internal storage are not applicable to this combinational driver; no state is modeled.
- `ASSUMPTION:` Analog bus voltage, output resistance, timing, and bus contention are outside this pure-digital model.
- All four native output-enable groups are retained independently.

## Verification

The dedicated testbench checks all 256 input patterns under all 16 OE combinations. Lint, simulation, and synthesis current-source results are published in the [library report](../../report/extended_report.md).

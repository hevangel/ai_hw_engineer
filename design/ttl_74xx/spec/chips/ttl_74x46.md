## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x46 — BCD to 7-segment decoder/driver, OC 30 V

Logic identical to `ttl_74x47`. The 74x46 supports higher output breakdown
voltage (30 V) for driving incandescent lamps; the electrical difference
is not modeled.

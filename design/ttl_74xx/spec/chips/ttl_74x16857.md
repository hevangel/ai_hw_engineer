# 74x16857: 14-bit registered buffer

**First introduced:** Unknown; the datasheet publication date is not evidence of
initial shipment. **Primary source:** [SN74SSTVF16857](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74sstvf16857.pdf), pages 1–2.
SHA-256: `9068369cfe88dbfcba99f6aa5da03b2ba5fde95d5df34024f716d4d717c13893`.

These parts support memory address/control distribution and wide board-level
datapaths. Their historical role includes registered DIMM and backplane interfaces.
The manufacturer's logical control table is retained; analog electrical behavior
and weak bus-hold circuitry are outside the pure-digital interface.

## Contract

`rst_n=0` asynchronously forces all outputs to zero and dominates the clock.
With reset released, rising `clk` captures `d[13:0]`; stable or falling clock
holds. The registered word appears on q, 14 bits.
ASSUMPTION: `clk` represents the manufacturer's valid differential crossing of CLK
high and its complement low. There is no output enable and no fabricated reset cycle.

ASSUMPTION: Inputs meet manufacturer setup/hold requirements; mode controls change
with data stable. Storage is unspecified before the first transparent load,
qualifying clock edge or native reset. A disabled output returns zero with its
drive indication low; consumers must use the drive indication to resolve the bus.

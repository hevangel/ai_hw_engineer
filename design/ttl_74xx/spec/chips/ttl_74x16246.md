# 74x16246: 11-bit incident-wave switching bus transceiver

First introduced: Unknown. [TI SN74ABTE16246 SCBS227J](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74abte16246.pdf), July 1993,
revised August 2003, establishes documentation dates rather than first shipment.
SHA-256: `b56d58e408f44a46562c07e4389c39d40539fcc9211612c3cd460530e6340080`. Function table PDF page 2; positive
logic diagram PDF page 3, visually reviewed. This part combined eight split-path
open-collector channels with three independently controlled bus transceivers,
supporting VME64 backplanes and high-current board interconnects.

## Native digital contract

`a_in[10:0]` observes 1A..11A, `bi_in[7:0]` observes 1BI..8BI, and
`b_in[2:0]` observes 9B..11B. `a_out/a_drive[10:0]` describes 1A..11A;
`b_out/b_drive[10:0]` describes 1BO..8BO then 9B..11B.
`dir[0..2]` maps 9DIR..11DIR; `oe_11_n` maps 11OE and `oe_n` maps OE.

OE high isolates every output. While OE is low:

- Channels 1..8 simultaneously transfer BI to A through **open-collector**
  outputs and A to BO through non-inverting three-state outputs. An input BI low
  sinks its A output; BI high releases A. A-side bus observations include actual
  resolved feedback, external pullups and other board drivers.
- Channels 9 and 10: DIR low transfers A to B, DIR high transfers B to A.
- Channel 11: DIR high transfers B to A regardless of 11OE. DIR low transfers
  A to B only while 11OE is low; 11OE high isolates that channel.

Each positive drive flag indicates a strong actively driven output. Released data
is masked to zero; a zero data value with drive false is high impedance.
There is no invented reset, clock or storage.

ASSUMPTION: Inputs are valid resolved digital levels on a powered device, with
settled controls. Analog bus-hold, VCCBIAS precharge, voltage thresholds, current,
series resistance, propagation delay and transient contention are omitted. Weak
keepers and pullups must be resolved by the board model before driving inputs.

## Contract

`a_bus[0]`..`a_bus[3]` map A1..A4, `b_bus[0]`..`b_bus[3]` map B1..B4, `a_aux[0]`..`a_aux[2]` map A5..A7, and `y_aux[0]`..`y_aux[2]` map Y5..Y7. `dir=0` selects B-to-A transfer: B1..B4 are inputs and A1..A4 are driven from B. `dir=1` selects A-to-B transfer: A1..A4 are inputs and B1..B4 are driven from A. The two direction modes are exclusive.

A5..A7 independently feed Y5..Y7 in either direction mode. `hd=1` selects push-pull output behavior on B1..B4 in A-to-B mode and on Y5..Y7. `hd=0` selects open-drain behavior on those output groups: an input low drives low; an input high is high-impedance. A1..A4 outputs in B-to-A mode are always push-pull. No output is driven in its disabled mode. Output data is masked to zero when its corresponding `drive` flag is low; consumers must honor the flags.

The RTL abstracts the source's open-drain mode as digital low-or-Z. It does not model analog voltage/current, input hysteresis, B-side overvoltage protection, IEEE 1284 electrical timing, output slew, supply behavior, or bus contention. The part is combinational and has no power-up state.

## Verification

The independent testbench covers all 16,384 combinations of direction, high-drive mode, both 4-bit bus inputs, and the 3-bit auxiliary input.

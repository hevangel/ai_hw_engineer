## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x180 — 9-bit even/odd parity generator and checker

Computes parity over 8 data bits with two control inputs (`even_in`,
`odd_in`) that select the desired output sense. Used both as a generator
(to produce a parity bit) and as a checker (to verify parity in a 9-bit
field).

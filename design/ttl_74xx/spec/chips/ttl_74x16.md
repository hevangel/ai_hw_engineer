## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x16 — Hex inverter buffer/driver, OC 15V/40mA

Same digital function as `ttl_74x04`. Output is open-collector with
15 V breakdown / 40 mA sink capability. Electrical drive not modeled.

Datasheet: [SN7416](https://www.ti.com/lit/gpn/sn7416).

# 74x16470: 16-bit registered bus transceiver

First introduced: Unknown. [TI SN74ABT16470 SCBS085E](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74abt16470.pdf), February 1991,
revised May 1997, is the primary source. Publication dates do not establish first
shipment. SHA-256: `75f4ad1ba4963ea4b195ba9fcfc7a7dd4bf8179b0fe84c80a4c2fb4ab6aebfc6`. Function table PDF page 2;
native pinout page 1; positive-logic diagram page 4 visually reviewed.

Two independent eight-bit sections allow registered transfers in both directions.
Separate clocks and enables make the part useful for pipelined board buses.

## Native digital contract

`a_in/b_in[15:0]` observes bank 1 pins 1A1..1A8 / 1B1..1B8 in bits 0..7,
bank 2 pins 2A1..2A8 / 2B1..2B8 in bits 8..15. Every two-bit control vector
uses bit 0 for bank 1 and bit 1 for bank 2. AB transfers A to B; BA transfers B
to A. `oe_ab_n`, `oe_ba_n`, `clken_ab_n`, `clken_ba_n` preserve native active-low
enables. `clk_ab`, `clk_ba` are the four native clocks.

Each register captures on a rising edge of its native gated clock
`CLK AND NOT CLKEN`. Thus CLKEN high stops capture and **also disables the output**,
irrespective of OE. With CLKEN low and OE low, the output shows the stored byte;
OE high releases the output independently of capture. Both directions can drive
simultaneously; resolve physical buses externally. Positive per-bank `a_drive`
and `b_drive` flags accompany zero-masked output data. Unreset storage starts
unspecified and must be initialized using native clocks.

ASSUMPTION: Follow the explicit source restriction: do not change CLKEN from high
to low while CLK is high. The modeled gate would otherwise create an extra clock
edge, as the native diagram does. Valid setup/hold and settled powered logic are
required. Analog thresholds, delays, drive strength, hazards and contention
resolution are omitted.

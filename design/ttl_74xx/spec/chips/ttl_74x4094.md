package-pin replica. Electrical families, propagation delays and analog effects

with its drive mask; split bus inputs/outputs require board-level resolution.

## Reference interface notes (MIT baseline)

# ttl_74x4094 — 8-bit 3-state shift register / latch

Serial shift on posedge `clk`, transparent storage latch on `stb`, and 3-state
parallel output gated by `oe`. `qs1` and `qs2` expose the shift-out MSB.

## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x41 — BCD-to-decimal decoder / Nixie tube driver

Logic identical to `ttl_74x42`. Output stage is open-collector 70 V for
driving Nixie tubes (cold-cathode neon display tubes). OC + HV drive
not modeled.

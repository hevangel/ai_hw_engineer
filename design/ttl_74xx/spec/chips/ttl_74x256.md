## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x256 — Dual 4-bit addressable latch

Two 4-bit latches with individual bit addressing. When `g_n=0`, the bit
addressed by `a` is updated from `d` for each latch.


## Local contract corrections

With clear low and enable low the selected output follows data and unselected outputs clear, implementing the datasheet decoder mode. With clear low and enable high all outputs clear. Each stored bit has its own decode/enable; no combinational mux feedback is used.

## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x19 — Hex Schmitt-trigger inverter

Logic identical to `ttl_74x14`. The 74x19 datasheet specifies a slightly
different pinout from 74x14 but the digital behaviour is the same. Schmitt
hysteresis not modeled.

## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x26 — Quad 2-input NAND, OC HV interface

Logic identical to `ttl_74x00`. The original part is an open-collector
high-voltage (15 V) interface NAND for relay and lamp driving. None of
the electrical drive characteristics are modeled.

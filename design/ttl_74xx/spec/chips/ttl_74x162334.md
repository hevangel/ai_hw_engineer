# 74x162334: 16-bit universal bus driver

**First introduced:** Unknown; the datasheet publication date is not evidence of
initial shipment. **Primary source:** [SN74ALVC162334](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74alvc162334.pdf), pages 1–3.
SHA-256: `697b2491553431537b1ff7f41c9ab8b2835bec0193e4c8c0d6bae47d4b6e8da6`.

These parts support memory address/control distribution and wide board-level
datapaths. Their historical role includes registered DIMM and backplane interfaces.
The manufacturer's logical control table is retained; analog electrical behavior
and weak bus-hold circuitry are outside the pure-digital interface.

## Contract

`a[15:0]` maps to A1 through A16; `y` maps to Y1 through Y16.
`oe_n=0` drives the outputs. `le=0` makes storage transparent,
independently of clock level. With the opposite LE level, rising `clk` captures A;
both stable clock levels and the falling edge hold. Output disable does not inhibit
storage. In the datasheet's qualified LE-change cases the clock must be high before
leaving transparency; tests obey that condition. `drive` is the positive output-enable.
The source's F or H family letter affects electrical drive/bus-hold only here.

ASSUMPTION: Inputs meet manufacturer setup/hold requirements; mode controls change
with data stable. Storage is unspecified before the first transparent load,
qualifying clock edge or native reset. A disabled output returns zero with its
drive indication low; consumers must use the drive indication to resolve the bus.

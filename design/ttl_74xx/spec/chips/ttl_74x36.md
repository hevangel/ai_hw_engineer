## Functional baseline and interface notes

# ttl_74x36 — Quad 2-input NOR, alternate pinout

Logic identical to `ttl_74x02`. The 74x36 differs only in its DIP pin
assignment. Since the RTL is in terms of `a`/`b`/`y` vectors, the difference
is only relevant when mapping the module to physical pins.

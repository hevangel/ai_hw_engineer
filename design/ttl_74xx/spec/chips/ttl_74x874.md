## Reference interface notes (MIT baseline)

# ttl_74x874 — Dual 4-bit edge-triggered D flip-flop with clear (3-state)

Edge-triggered companion of [ttl_74x873](ttl_74x873.md): each
4-bit unit captures D on the posedge of its own `clk`, with an async
active-low `/CLR` and an output-only `/OE`.

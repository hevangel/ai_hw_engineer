## Reference interface notes (MIT baseline)

# ttl_74x5074 — Dual positive-edge D-FF (metastable-immune)

Two independent D flip-flops with async preset/clear and an internal
dual-rank synchronizer per FF to reduce metastability on the data input.
Each output reaches its new value after two clocks.
